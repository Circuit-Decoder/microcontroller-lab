# practice_4_speed_test

**Task:** Speed test: find the shortest delay at which you can still see E1.4 blinking. Write the number in your project log.

**What you should find:** most people can still see flicker at about 20–30 ms (on for 20 ms, off for 20 ms = 25 blinks a second). Below about 10–15 ms the LED looks steadily on, just a little dimmer, because your eyes cannot follow more than about 50–60 flashes a second. The exact number differs from person to person, and flicker is easier to see out of the corner of your eye or when you move your head. Write your own number in your project log.
