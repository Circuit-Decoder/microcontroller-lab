# bonus_2_exact_masses

**Task:** Exact masses: replace the rounded masses with exact ones (H = 1.008, O = 15.999…) and show 3 decimals. How much do the answers change?

How much do the answers change? Very little: water goes from 18 to 18.015 g/mol, CO2 from 44 to 44.009, glucose from 180 to 180.156. Rounded masses are fine for school sums; exact ones matter in real laboratories. Some lines get long: "H: 12 x 1.008 = 12.096" just fits in 21 characters.
